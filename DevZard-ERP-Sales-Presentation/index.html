The problem

Schools are running on fragments.

Manual paperwork

Admission files, attendance registers, and fee books live in different rooms.

Disconnected tools

WhatsApp for parents, Excel for fees, a website that never talks to the office.

Fee leakage

Challans issued by hand. Paid vs unpaid is a weekly reconstruction.

Attendance drift

Student and teacher presence is hard to prove when a parent calls.

Result delays

Marks sit with teachers. Families wait. Countdowns are improvised.

Many campuses, no centre

Each branch invents its own process. Head office cannot see one picture.

“One platform. One system. Complete control.”

The DevZard answer

The entire school operation, in one product.

  • Centralized student, staff, fee, and academic records
  • Role-based portals — each person sees only their work
  • Multi-campus schools with branch isolation
  • Secure sign-in and admin-controlled accounts
  • Live operational data instead of weekly recaps
  • Digital workflows for admissions, fees, and results
  • Parent and student communication in the same system
  • Modules you can switch on as the school is ready
